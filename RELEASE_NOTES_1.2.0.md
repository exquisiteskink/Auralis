# Auralis 1.2.0

- **Offline listening:** download albums or playlists and play saved tracks from **Home → Downloads**, including after an offline restart. Includes Wi-Fi-only downloads and storage controls.
- **Sleep timer:** stop after 15 / 30 / 45 / 60 minutes or at the end of the current track. Works with crossfade cancellation and explicit playback controls.
- **Android Auto browsing:** playlists, recently played albums, favorites, and recently added albums. Phone-originated queues and duplicate playlist entries are preserved.
- **Now Playing:** displays bit depth and sample rate from the current song's server-provided metadata, such as `24-bit · 96 kHz`; missing values are omitted.
- Includes the artwork override scaffold; a user-facing picker is not included yet.

Download `Auralis-1.2.0.apk` below. Android 8.0 or later is required. The APK uses the same signing certificate as 1.1.6 for in-place updates.

Device and Android Auto head-unit testing were not performed. Android Auto voice search is not included.
