# Poweramp Equalizer DVC playback guard

Poweramp describes Direct Volume Control (DVC) as a more direct output volume path with more EQ headroom. Its guidance also warns about Bluetooth Absolute Volume and enabling DVC in both Poweramp apps. See [Poweramp's DVC guide](https://forum.powerampapp.com/kb/en_us/guides/using-direct-volume-control-dvc-r42/).

Poweramp's developers recommend one audio session for the player lifetime. Auralis allocates a sticky session, opens it for external effects before playback, and closes it on service destruction. See the [Poweramp Equalizer discussion](https://forum.powerampapp.com/topic/21624-poweramp-equalizer-stops-processing-audio-after-some-time/#findComment-102916).

Media3 1.5.1 releases its AudioTrack when `DefaultAudioSink.flush()` runs. A seek can therefore create a new AudioTrack even though the audio session ID stays the same. Auralis mutes before controller seeks and queue replacement, keeps the mute while paused, and starts the settle timer after Media3 reports a new AudioTrack for a guarded seek. It then ramps player volume back up. On errors, Auralis stays muted through retry.

On a Galaxy Z Fold 6 running Android 16, the system audio log showed a new AudioTrack starting 31–47 ms before its client volume became muted. A decoded PCM gate alone still produced a brief burst, including on pause. Priming new AudioTracks at zero volume also burst. Pre-muting the current AudioTrack and waiting 70 ms before Pause burst on three of five tries, even though `AudioTrack.setVolume(0)` was logged before Pause. A silent streaming AudioTrack on the same sticky session remained active during pause; the five-cycle phone test had no bursts. A static AudioTrack prototype failed to initialize on the Fold 6 and was discarded.

The current guard uses the silent anchor and a PCM gate. For a quick Pause/Resume while the anchor remains active, Auralis leaves the audible AudioTrack's queued samples and the gate intact. This avoids the brief old-audio playback followed by silence and a fade seen in the first anchor build. While the anchor is warm and PCM is already audible, Auralis also keeps the gate open through seek, manual skip, album replacement, and automatic item transitions. The Fold 6 listening test found no bursts and natural transitions on quick Pause/Resume, manual skips, seeks, and album changes. After 15 seconds paused, Auralis closes the PCM gate and mutes the dormant AudioTrack before stopping the anchor; a later Resume restarts the anchor and waits 450 ms before restoring sound. Seeking during pause also uses that guarded path. The PCM ramp after a guarded transition is 80 ms. ReplayGain is applied to decoded PCM when a known external EQ is installed, so Auralis does not compete with Poweramp for the platform DynamicsProcessing effect. The PCM gate is disabled when no known external EQ is installed.

When Poweramp Equalizer or another known external EQ is installed, Auralis disables dual-player crossfade. With a warm anchor it allows normal single-player automatic item transitions. If the anchor is unavailable, it uses `pauseAtEndOfMediaItems` and advances the queue while muted. The setting may still show crossfade enabled, but overlap does not run in this mode.

There is no public API to read Poweramp Equalizer's active DVC setting or to learn when it has rebound to a new AudioTrack. Package detection is a conservative proxy. The 450 ms bind wait is a heuristic; a device listening test is required to check for bursts, the restored level, and playback behavior. Test seeking, scrubbing, pause/resume, manual and automatic next, replacing an album queue, repeat, sleep timer, and Bluetooth routing at a low hardware volume first. Capture `Auralis/DvcSession` logs with any remaining spike.
